Business Model

Independent Power Producer

PSA Renewables operates under India's IPP framework, generating clean power through privately owned assets and selling it under long-term contracts.

  1. Project Development

    Identifying, permitting, and structuring greenfield renewable energy projects.

  2. Power Purchase

    Securing long-term PPAs with DISCOMs and industrial consumers at competitive tariffs.

  3. Asset Ownership

    Owning and operating assets with professional in-house O&M for 25+ year lifespans.

  4. Revenue Generation

    Generating stable, long-term cash flows from power sales under solid contracts.
